A simple workflow

From student record to student portal.

Here is the intended process for an administrator-created student account.

01

Admin Adds a Student

The administrator enters the student's personal and academic details into the management dashboard.

02

Account Is Prepared

The student record is associated with a user account according to the application's workflow.

03

Password Setup Email

The student receives the account activation email containing the password setup link.

04

Student Signs In

After setting a password, the student signs in and accesses their own dashboard.
